# The Salty Vibe Blog Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach** - Drawing inspiration from lifestyle blogs like Goop, Cup of Jo, and coastal brands like Anthropologie. The design emphasizes visual storytelling, feminine aesthetics, and coastal charm to create an inviting space for lifestyle, travel, and food content.

## Core Design Elements

### Color Palette
**Light Mode:**
- Primary: 340 65% 75% (coral pink)
- Secondary: 180 35% 80% (soft seafoam)
- Accent: 45 25% 85% (sandy beige)
- Text: 220 15% 25% (charcoal)
- Background: 0 0% 98% (soft white)

**Dark Mode:**
- Primary: 340 45% 65% (muted coral)
- Secondary: 180 25% 40% (deep teal)
- Accent: 45 15% 35% (warm taupe)
- Text: 0 0% 90% (light gray)
- Background: 220 15% 12% (deep charcoal)

### Typography
- **Headers:** Google Fonts "Playfair Display" (elegant serif for titles)
- **Subheaders:** Google Fonts "Poppins" Medium (clean sans-serif)
- **Body:** Google Fonts "Source Sans Pro" (readable, friendly)

### Layout System
Tailwind spacing primitives: 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16
- Consistent rhythm using p-4, m-6, gap-8
- Section spacing: py-12 or py-16
- Card padding: p-6
- Button spacing: px-6 py-3

### Component Library
- **Navigation:** Clean header with script logo, horizontal nav links
- **Cards:** Rounded corners (rounded-xl), subtle shadows, hover lift effects
- **Buttons:** Soft rounded (rounded-lg), coral primary, outline variants for hero overlays with backdrop blur
- **Forms:** Soft inputs with focus states in coral
- **Blog Posts:** Featured image cards with overlay text, reading time badges

### Visual Treatments
**Gradients:** Subtle coral-to-seafoam gradients for hero sections and accent areas (340 65% 75% to 180 35% 80%)

**Background Treatments:** Soft, organic shapes and gentle textures suggesting sand and water. Light coral and seafoam color blocks for section dividers.

## Images
**Hero Image:** Large lifestyle hero showcasing coastal living - sunhat, coffee, ocean view, soft natural lighting
**Blog Cards:** High-quality lifestyle photography - food flatlays, travel destinations, fashion details
**About Section:** Personal portrait in coastal setting with warm, natural lighting
**Category Icons:** Minimalist line icons in coral pink for lifestyle, travel, and food sections

## Key Features
- Featured blog post carousel with large images
- Category filtering with gentle color coding
- Social sharing with custom coral-themed icons
- Search with elegant dropdown styling
- Newsletter signup with coastal-inspired background
- Responsive grid layouts showcasing visual content

The overall aesthetic balances feminine sophistication with coastal relaxation, creating an aspirational yet approachable space for lifestyle content consumption.
